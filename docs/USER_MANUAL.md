## 6. Setup & Connection

### Wireless Connection (2.4 GHz Mode)
1. Remove the 2.4 GHz USB nano receiver from the compartment on the underside of the mouse.
2. Insert the receiver into an available USB 2.0 or 3.0 port on the computer.
3. Slide the power switch on the bottom of the mouse to **ON**.
4. The device connects automatically via standard USB HID plug-and-play.

### Wired Mode & Charging
1. Connect the provided Type-C cable to the front port of the mouse and the opposite USB-A end to your computer.
2. To use strictly in wired mode, remove the wireless receiver from your computer.
3. **Low Battery Indicator:** When the LED beneath the scroll wheel blinks **red**, the battery is depleted. Connect the Type-C cable to recharge the mouse.
4. **Charging Status:** The scroll wheel LED indicates active charging state until full battery capacity is restored.

---

## 7. Controls & Lighting Operation

### Power & LED Switches
* **Power Switch:** Slide to toggle power **ON** or **OFF**.
* **LED Mode Switch:**
  * **Short Press:** Cycle through 6 spectrum lighting modes sequentially.
  * **Press & Hold:** Toggle the RGB lighting completely **ON** or **OFF**.

## 8. Software Configuration

Official configuration software is available from [www.fantechworld.com](https://www.fantechworld.com). The application provides the following controls:

### Key Settings
* **Button Assignment:** Remap all 6 buttons to custom functions, multimedia actions, Windows shortcuts, or macro sequences.
* **Profiles:** Store and switch up to 4 distinct on-board memory profiles (`Profile 1` .. `Profile 4`).
* **Debounce Time:** Configurable switch debounce delay from **0 ms to 30 ms** to eliminate accidental double clicks.

### Advanced Settings
* **DPI Stages:** Configure up to 6 custom DPI stages (hardware supports up to 7 active stages) ranging from **50 DPI to 19,000 DPI** in 50 DPI increments ($\le 10,000$) or 100 DPI increments ($> 10,000$).
* **Lift-Off Distance (LOD):** Selectable between **1 mm** and **2 mm**.
* **Sensor Tuning:** Ripple control (jitter smoothing) and Angle Snapping (FixLine).

### Macro Setup
#### Recording a New Macro
1. Open the Macro tab and click the **"New Macro"** button.
2. Enter the desired **Macro Name**.
3. Click **"Start recording"** to input keystrokes and mouse button clicks.
4. Delay intervals and cycle repeat counts can be edited manually.
5. Click **"Stop record"**, then click **"Save"** to store the macro sequence.

#### Assigning a Macro to a Button
1. Return to the **Key Settings** panel.
2. Select the button to assign.
3. Choose **Macro** from the dropdown and select the saved **Macro Name**.
4. Click **OK** / **Apply** to save changes to the mouse memory.

### Lighting Setup
* **7 Spectrum Modes:**
  1. Steady
  2. Breathing
  3. Streaming
  4. Neon
  5. Scrolling
  6. Colourful Breathing
  7. LED OFF
* **Adjustable Parameters:** Adjust brightness (10 steps) and effect animation speed (10 steps) for supported modes.
* **Auto-Sleep Timer:** Set idle sleep interval from **10 seconds to 40 minutes** to preserve wireless battery life.
